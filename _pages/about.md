By profession, I am a scientist interested in how experience changes how people make decisions (e.g. learning and decision making). Lately, I have been thinking how people construct the problems they want to solve in the first place. If I wanted to set a New Year's resolution, how might I go about formulating it? Or if I wanted to make a game, how might I go about building one? I typically use computational tools from reinforcement learning and Bayesian inference to understand how people address these questions. 

In my personal life, I'm interested in games, mentoring, and building community. I'm an avid Game Master playing games like Dungeons & Dragons.
